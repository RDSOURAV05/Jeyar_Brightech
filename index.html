Common Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about grid synchronization, battery backup, or structural installations? We have answers.

For standard residential structures (3kW to 10kW), the physical installation of structural frames, wiring, and panel arrays takes 1 to 2 days. The net-metering approval and solar integration with the local electric board (EB) may take an additional 10 to 15 days depending on local regulatory processing timelines.

Yes, solar panels still generate power under cloudy, overcast, or rainy skies, as they utilize ambient daylight rather than just direct sunlight. Output will drop to about 10% to 25% of their sunny-day peak capacity. For backup, we offer hybrid inverter configurations tied to modular lithium energy storage.

Solar arrays require very low maintenance. The primary task is keeping panels clean from heavy dust, bird droppings, or foliage. We recommend a gentle rinse and wiping down every 2 to 4 weeks. Jeyar Brightech also offers annual maintenance contracts (AMCs) which include structural inspections, string checks, and infrared thermal imaging audits.

Net-metering measures the bidirectional flow of electricity. When your panels produce excess power during midday peak sun, the surplus electricity feeds back into the local grid. Your utility meter runs backward, earning you energy credits that offset any power you pull from the grid during nighttime hours.
