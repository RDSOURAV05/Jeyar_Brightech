Common Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about grid synchronization, government subsidies, or structural installations? We have answers.

For standard residential structures (3kW to 10kW), the physical installation of structural frames, wiring, and panel arrays takes 1 to 2 days. The net-metering approval and solar integration with the local electric board (KSEB in Kerala) may take an additional 10 to 15 days depending on local regulatory processing timelines.

For residential solar installations in Thiruvananthapuram, you can receive government subsidies up to ₹78,000 depending on the plant capacity. Our team handles the subsidy application paperwork directly, ensuring a smooth approval process with the central government portal.

We assist you in procuring solar loan facilities of up to ₹2,00,000 at a competitive 6% interest rate. This ensures that you can switch to solar energy with minimal upfront investment and let your monthly power bill savings pay back the loan.

All our major packages offer long-term warranties for peace of mind. For example, our solar panels come with a 30-year manufacturer warranty, inverters feature 10 years of warranty support, and boards/cables carry 5 to 10 years of support. We assist with all after-sales warranty claims directly.
